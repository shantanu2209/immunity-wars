# The hint splits, for Kartik to correct

**9 September 2026.** Every one of these is a **proposal**, and correcting a proposal is much
faster than deciding a cut from nothing, which is why they are here rather than as a list of
questions. Change any of them.

## What this is for, in one paragraph

When a player meets something for the first time — the first time they *tap* a Neutrophil, not
the first time one is on the board — the app will show one short line saying what it is. The
words are not new. **They are the first part of the words already in How to play.** So each entry
below is cut into two: the **hint**, which is what the player sees at that moment, and the
**rest**, which follows it in How to play exactly as it does today.

**How to play does not change by a single character.** It renders hint and rest together, and a
test requires the joined text to be byte-identical to what is there now. If a cut is in the wrong
place, How to play still reads the same; only the hint is wrong.

**Nothing is duplicated.** The hint is not a shorter copy of the long text, it is the opening of
it. There is one set of words and it cannot drift, because there is nothing to drift from.

## How to read the tables

The full entry is shown with `▌` marking the cut. Everything before it is the hint; everything
after is the rest.

**Seventeen splits, not eighteen.** There are eighteen entries, but one of them
(`help.cell.resident.name`) is the heading "Resident macrophages" and is not prose, so there is
nothing to cut.

## The eleven clean cuts

These fall on a sentence boundary and the hint stands on its own. They need a glance, not a
decision.

### Neutrophil
> NET: traps every living microbe on its own space. ▌ Then it is spent and returns after 4 turns,
> or 2 if a primed Helper T-Cell is in the bloodstream.

### B-Cell
> Never moves. Produce antibodies of one antigen class. ▌ Coat a bacterium, worm or parasite.
> Neutralise a virus, or a toxin for 2 AP. Vaccinate, 5 AP in total. Search for the clone, 3 AP,
> for Pathogen X.

*"Never moves" leads, which reads oddly in a long entry and reads well in a hint: it is the thing
about the B-Cell a player cannot see and will otherwise discover by trying.*

### Killer T-Cell
> Snipe: destroys a pathogen hiding inside one of your cells. Never misses. ▌ Range 3 on Training,
> 2 on Normal and Hard, one more while a primed Helper stands with it.

### NK Cell
> NK strike: attacks a hidden or infected cell within one step, on a die roll of 3 or more. ▌
> Needs no antigen and no antibody.

### Eosinophil
> Strike: 2 damage to a coated worm or parasite. ▌ Degranulate, 2 AP: 3 damage, enough to kill a
> worm outright, but it burns the organ it stands in and the cell is spent for 4 turns.

*The hint leaves out degranulate, which is the dramatic half. At first contact the question is
"what is this cell for", and the answer is worms and parasites.*

### Resident macrophages
> One lives in each organ. Patrol: 1 AP per step along its own organ branch. ▌ It may never leave
> that branch, and it must be out on the branch to meet anything: nothing can be engulfed in the
> organ box itself. Engulf, free once per turn: destroy one virus or coated bacterium on its space.

⚠️ *A judgement call worth your eye. **"It may never leave that branch" may be the more useful
first thing** than the AP cost of patrolling, since it is the rule a player will otherwise break
before learning. Moving the cut one sentence later makes the hint three sentences and about 200
characters, which is long. Your call.*

### Virus
> Neutralise with a matching antibody, or the Monocyte engulfs it. ▌ If it hides inside a cell,
> only the Killer T-Cell or NK Cell can reach it.

### Hidden
> It is inside one of your own cells, where antibodies cannot go. Killer T-Cell (never misses) or
> NK Cell (3 or more). ▌ Not all are viruses: Toxoplasmosis and Chagas are protozoa that live
> inside cells.

*The hint carries the problem and the answer together, which is what makes it usable alone.*

### Bacteria
> Coat it with a matching antibody, then engulf it, or trap the swarm in a NET. It divides if you
> ignore it. ▌ Up to 8 can pack into one space.

*The cut is after two sentences rather than one, because "it divides if you ignore it" is the
consequence of doing nothing, and a hint that omits it invites doing nothing.*

### Worm
> Too big to swallow and immune to NETs. Coat it, then the Eosinophil strikes (2) or degranulates
> (3). ▌ It lodges in an organ and chews it every 3 turns.

### Parasite
> Coat it, then strike it down: Eosinophil 2 damage, Monocyte 1. ▌ Once it is on its last hit
> point the Monocyte can finally swallow it.

## The three where the whole entry is the hint

These are already short, and the first sentence alone says only what does **not** work. A hint
that says "it cannot be coated" and stops leaves a player with no move. So the whole entry is the
hint and there is no rest. How to play is unchanged: it renders the hint and nothing follows.

### Fungus — whole entry, no rest
> It cannot be coated. The Monocyte engulfs it directly (2 hits, so that chips it) and a NET kills
> it outright.

### Toxin — whole entry, no rest
> Not alive: nothing can eat it, trap it or snipe it. Antitoxin antibodies only, and neutralising
> costs 2 AP.

### Venom — whole entry, no rest
> Not alive, and far too fast for your B-cells. Only a ready-made antivenom dose works.

## The two long ones, where the honest cut is longer than the rule

### Monocyte — hint is two sentences, 155 characters
> Engulf: free once a turn, then 1 AP. Swallows a virus, a coated bacterium, a fungus, blood-stage
> malaria, or a parasite already down to its last hit point. ▌ Strike: 1 damage to a coated worm
> or parasite.

*Cutting after the first sentence gives "Engulf: free once a turn, then 1 AP.", which is a price
with no product: it never says that engulfing is how the Monocyte kills things. The list is what
makes the sentence mean something.*

### Malaria — hint is four sentences, 153 characters
> Three stages. Travelling in the blood: antibodies or the Monocyte. Inside liver cells: Killer
> T-Cell or NK Cell only. Back in the blood: antibodies again. ▌ The card has the stages.

*This one cannot be cut inside the list. "Three stages." then one of them is worse than saying
nothing, because it announces a count and then does not deliver it. The whole list is the unit.*

## The one that needs REWRITING rather than cutting

⚠️ **This is a different ask from the rest of this document, and it is yours to decide.**

### Helper T-Cell — no cut works

> Licenses the others, but only once it has been primed: an antigen must have been presented,
> which happens the first time any of your cells engulfs or destroys a pathogen. With the B-Cell:
> one more antibody per action. With the Killer T-Cell: one more range. With the Eosinophil: one
> more step. In the bloodstream: the Neutrophil returns in 2 turns.

**The first sentence is 170 characters and it is one sentence.** There is nowhere to cut it. The
only cut inside it lands on the colon, which would make the hint end mid-thought.

So this one needs **new words**, not a cut. My suggestion, offered as a starting point rather than
a proposal, because these are words you did not write:

> **Hint:** It kills nothing. It licenses your other cells, and only after it has been primed.
>
> **Rest:** Priming means an antigen has been presented, which happens the first time any of your
> cells engulfs or destroys a pathogen. With the B-Cell: one more antibody per action. With the
> Killer T-Cell: one more range. With the Eosinophil: one more step. In the bloodstream: the
> Neutrophil returns in 2 turns.

**This changes what How to play says**, unlike every other entry here, so it is the one place
where the "How to play does not change" promise does not hold. The mechanic is identical; the
sentence is not. If you would rather keep your sentence exactly, the alternative is a 170
character hint, which is about four lines on a phone.

## Two subjects with no text at all, which is writing rather than cutting

The set Shantanu ruled includes two things How to play covers in passing but has no per-thing
entry for. These are new sentences and need your eye as writing.

### Antibody classes must match — proposed new text
> **Hint:** An antibody only fits its own antigen class. Making the wrong one is wasted work.
>
> This is taken almost word for word from How to play section 7, which already says it. No rest.

### The window closing — ⚠️ NOT PROPOSED, and there is a problem with it

The idea is that surviving to the end is not the win. **But the trigger cannot work.** A hint
fires on first CONTACT, and the window is not a thing a player touches: it is a number in the
bar. Under the rule as ruled, a player never encounters it, so the hint would never fire.

Rather than invent a trigger, this is reported. Three ways out, for Shantanu rather than for you:
make the bar's turn indicator tappable, move this one line into the difficulty screen where it is
read before the game starts, or drop it. **Nothing is built for it.**
